Development preview: Secure sign-in is connected. Other modules, data processing, approvals, and notifications may still be under development.

Welcome to Project e-NEXUS

A proposed digital platform for coordinating school services, tracking documents, monitoring projects, and improving transactions among schools and authorized partner agencies in Aliaga District, Nueva Ecija.

System Modules

📄

Document Routing and Tracking

Track submitted documents, assigned offices, review status, approvals, and release records.

📊

Project Monitoring and Evaluation

Organize project proposals, milestones, progress reports, accomplishments, and evaluation records.

📝

Online Requests and Approvals

Provide a structured process for submitting requests and recording authorized decisions.

📢

Announcements and Circulars

Organize official announcements, memoranda, circulars, and school advisories.

💬

Stakeholder Inquiries

Provide a channel for authorized users and community partners to submit inquiries and track responses.

🔐

Secure User Access

Planned role-based access for district administrators, school administrators, school personnel, and approved partners.

Access and Account Security

Accounts will be created or approved by authorized administrators. Access to school records and system functions will depend on assigned roles and permissions.

Account registration and login are not available in this preview.
